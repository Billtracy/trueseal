🧠 AI Forensic Engine — Two-Layer Analysis
Visual
Error Level Analysis (ELA)
Re-saves as JPEG and diffs compression artifacts. Digitally spliced names, dates, or grades produce bright red hotspots on the forensic heatmap — invisible to the human eye but detectable at the pixel level.
Textual
OCR + Alignment Detection
Tesseract extracts text with bounding boxes. Detects candidate name presence, vertical alignment anomalies from spliced text, and cross-references text regions with ELA compression hotspots.
Composite Score (0–100): ELA (50%) + OCR (50%) weighted. Score ≥ 40 = FAIL. Critical override: ELA hotspots overlapping text regions force a minimum score of 60.
